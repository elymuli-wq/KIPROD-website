John Martin Ambetsa is a seasoned banking, credit risk, and institutional strategy professional with over 25 years of experience across commercial banking, international banking, institutional advisory, and financial sector capability development.

He has worked with institutions including Co-operative Bank of Kenya and HSBC (UK), with experience spanning credit risk governance, portfolio monitoring, restructuring and recovery, trade finance, treasury operations, institutional strengthening, and financial sector advisory.

Within the KIPROD ecosystem, he leads institutional strategy, governance visibility engagements, ecosystem development, capability architecture, portfolio resilience initiatives, and institutional strengthening programmes. He has led multiple assignments across East Africa focused on credit risk systems strengthening, portfolio performance improvement, recovery and restructuring strategy, governance alignment, and institutional capability development.
