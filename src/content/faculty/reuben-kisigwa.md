Reuben Kisigwa is a highly experienced banking and credit risk professional with over 25 years of practical banking experience across corporate banking, SME lending, portfolio management, recovery strategy, and credit risk leadership.

He has held senior leadership positions at KCB Bank Kenya and Guaranty Trust Bank Kenya, including Senior Credit Manager, Area Manager – Business Banking, and Head of Credit Risk Management. His experience includes managing corporate and SME credit portfolios, restructuring distressed facilities, recovery strategy execution, credit risk policy implementation, portfolio risk management, and borrower engagement and negotiation.

Within the KIPROD ecosystem, he supports operational credit risk strengthening, recovery governance discussions, portfolio risk visibility, restructuring strategy, practical banking simulations, and executive and management capability development.
