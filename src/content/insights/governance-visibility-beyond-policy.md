Many financial institutions invest significantly in credit policy frameworks. Yet portfolio challenges persist where **governance visibility** is limited.

Governance visibility means leadership can see—clearly and consistently—how credit risk is:

- **Monitored** across the portfolio
- **Escalated** when triggers are breached
- **Discussed** in structured forums with decisions recorded
- **Acted on** through engagement, restructuring, and recovery

Without this visibility, policy exists on paper while execution diverges in practice.

### A practical starting point

Rather than expanding policy volume, institutions benefit from defining:

- A small set of **high-signal monitoring triggers**
- **Governance routines** (who meets, when, what gets decided)
- **Escalation paths** with owners and timelines
- **Recovery playbooks** teams can follow under pressure

This aligns directly with the KIPROD engagement model—building capability first, then strengthening governance visibility and operational execution.
