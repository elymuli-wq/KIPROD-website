and progression pathways.

- Primary text block (headline, subtext, CTAs)
- Optional supporting visual (pathway / ecosystem summary — not marketing clutter)
- Executive tone; premium spacing; no fintech hype

Content:

Headline:
Strengthening Credit Risk. Building Resilient Institutions.

Subtext:
From institutional capability development to governance visibility, operational
strengthening, and long-term portfolio resilience.

Executive insight line (may sit in hero or flow directly into Section 2):

visibility, structured systems, and disciplined execution.

CRITICAL DESIGN & CONTENT RULES
================================================================================

1. ALWAYS show TWO PATHWAYS (Capability + Institutional) — especially in hero.

2. Brand language (use → avoid):
   Capability Development        not  Training
   Institutional Strengthening   not  Consulting
   Governance Visibility         not  Diagnostics
   Institutional Partners        not  Clients
   Capability Platform           not  Online Courses

3. Site must feel:
   executive, institutional, governance-oriented, operationally intelligent,
   strategically mature, premium, structured

4. Site must NOT feel:
   startup, aggressive marketing, fintech hype, training company, consultancy
   brochure, services catalogue

5. One message per section. Generous white space. No clutter.

6. Downloads belong on /resources only — not on the homepage.
